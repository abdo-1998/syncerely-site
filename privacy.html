Syncerely Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Syncerely ("the app") is made by Abdo's Dev Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos and videos go from your phone to your own cloud accounts and back. They never pass through us, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

What the app works with, and where it goes

Your photos, videos and other files. The app reads the folders you pick on your phone and copies them to the cloud accounts you connect (Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive, Google Drive), or from those accounts to your phone. Files travel directly between your phone and that cloud company, over encrypted connections. We have no server that sees them, and we never receive a copy. What each cloud company does with your files is covered by its own privacy policy.

Photo location. Photos can hold the place they were taken. The app asks for access to this only so your photos keep it when they're copied to your cloud, unchanged. The app doesn't read or use the location itself.

Your cloud sign-ins. When you connect a cloud, you sign in on that company's own page; the app never sees your password. The cloud gives the app a sign-in key (a token), which is kept on your phone, encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware. The app uses it only to sync the folders you picked. Signing out of a cloud in the app deletes it from your phone.

Sync records. To know what has already synced, the app keeps a list of file names, sizes, dates and checksums on your phone, plus an activity log of what each sync did. These stay on your phone and are not included in Android's cloud backups.

The app trash. Files a sync removes from your phone are kept for a while in the app's own private storage, so you can bring them back. Only the app can see them.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and Lifetime are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. The app asks Google Play which plan you have, and keeps that answer on your phone so syncs work offline.

Crash reports (off unless you turn them on)

If you turn on crash reports in Settings, the app sends a report to our crash reporting service, Sentry (sentry.io), when it crashes. Before a report leaves your phone, the app removes file and folder names, paths, email addresses and sign-in keys from it. A report holds the error, the app version, your phone model and Android version. Reports are used only to fix bugs and are deleted after 30 days. You can turn them off at any time.

Messages from us

While the app is open, and now and then in the background, it reads a small public file we publish for important notices (for example, "please update, a bug can affect your sync"). Nothing about you is sent with it; like any web request, it reaches the host (GitHub Pages) with your IP address, which GitHub may log.

Feedback

If you use Suggest a feature or Report a problem in Settings, what you write is sent to us through Sentry (sentry.io), the same service as crash reports, but only when you tap Send, whether crash reports are on or not. A problem report can add a short summary (app version, phone model, Android version, plan, how many pairs per cloud, how many files keep failing); it's shown in full and you can untick it. No file, folder or account names are included. If you give your email for a reply, it's sent with your message. We use what you send only to answer you and improve the app, and it is deleted after 30 days, unless we're still writing back to you.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking or analytics.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information.
  • We don't build a profile of you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it, including the app trash. Your files in your clouds stay where they are. To have any crash reports about you deleted, or for any question, write to abditani.98@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
